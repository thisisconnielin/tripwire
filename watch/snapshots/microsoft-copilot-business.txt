01/
What is Microsoft Copilot and how is it different from Copilot Chat?
Microsoft Copilot is an AI-powered assistant that helps you work more efficiently by generating content, answering questions, analyzing information, and automating tasks. It combines large language models with your work data from Microsoft Graph and Microsoft 365 to provide personalized assistance. Copilot Chat is a web-grounded AI assistant included at no additional cost with eligible Microsoft 365 plans. It also includes Copilot in Outlook and the Microsoft Copilot app. Customers can use custom agents built by their organization on a metered basis.
When you purchase a Microsoft 365 plan with Copilot, or add a Microsoft 365 Copilot license to an existing subscription, you unlock additional capabilities, including Work IQ, Copilot in Teams and Microsoft 365 apps such as Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, access to Microsoft pre-built agents like Researcher, Analyst, and Facilitator, and the ability to use assistive custom agents at no additional cost. Organizations with Microsoft 365 Copilot licenses also gain access to advanced analytics to measure usage, adoption, and business impact.
02/
Which apps support Microsoft Copilot and what can it do in each?
Copilot delivers AI assistance tailored to the Microsoft 365 apps. For example, in Word, it helps draft documents, suggest edits, and summarize text. In Excel, it analyzes data, creates charts, and generates insights. In PowerPoint, it helps design presentations by generating outlines and recommending images or layouts. In Outlook, it drafts emails and summarizes lengthy email threads. In Teams, it recaps meetings and highlights action items from chats.
03/
What Microsoft Copilot features are usage-based?
Everyday AI experiences, like chat and Copilot in your Microsoft 365 apps, are included with your license. More advanced AI experiences are billed based on usage. Today, these include Copilot Cowork, Code, Autopilot, and the Work IQ APIs. More usage-based services may be added over time.
04/
How does usage-based billing work?
Usage-based billing lets you pay for advanced AI capabilities based on how much you use them. These experiences use Copilot Credits, so you pay based on actual usage, while the Copilot capabilities included with your subscription stay included. Learn more about usage-based billing and Copilot Credits.
Learn more about usage-based billing and Copilot Credits.
05/
How do I control what my organization spends?
Administrators can manage usage-based billing in Cost Management in the Microsoft 365 admin center. You can adjust usage controls and spending policies to fit your organization’s needs. Charges apply only when usage-based experiences are used.

How does Microsoft Copilot help support data security and privacy?
Microsoft Copilot is built with enterprise-grade security and privacy, using the same controls and permissions that help protect your data in Microsoft 365. Copilot follows your organization’s existing Microsoft 365 security settings.
With enterprise data protection (EDP) in Copilot, prompts and responses stay within the Microsoft 365 service boundary and all associated data handling commitments apply, including support for General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), International Organization for Standardization (ISO)/IEC 27018, and the Microsoft Data Protection Addendum (DPA). Prompts and responses are logged, subject to your data retention policies, and will be available for eDiscovery and other Microsoft Purview capabilities. The specific controls will vary depending on the underlying subscription plan. Prompts and responses are not used to train the underlying large language models.

What are the main benefits of Microsoft Copilot for business?
Microsoft Copilot enhances business processes by combining large language models (LLMs) with your work context. Key features include:
Microsoft Graph Grounding and Microsoft Graph connectors: Adds context from emails, chats, documents, and meetings.​
Enterprise Data Protection (EDP): Ensures data stays within Microsoft 365 boundaries, supporting GDPR and other standards.​
Copilot Chat: Integrates web, work, and business data into a rich information database.​
Copilot Pages: Organizes and shares content for easy collaboration and project tracking.​
Copilot in the flow of your work: Available in Microsoft 365 apps like Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook.​
Microsoft Teams collaboration: Streamlines meetings with real-time summaries and insights.​
Pre-built agents: Connects to business applications, including SharePoint.​
Copilot Studio: Enables users to create and customize workflow agents using the Copilot Chat or Standard harness.​
IT controls: Helps manage AI at scale including management controls, measurement, and reporting.

What is Work IQ and how does it work?
Work IQ is the intelligence layer that enriches Microsoft Copilot and agents with an implicit understanding of your work—inside and out. It connects your context, workflows, and relationships to deliver an experience that is built just for you and the flow of your work. Work IQ includes data from multiple sources: Microsoft 365 content and metadata; line of business data ingested through Copilot connectors—and the comprehensive semantic index derived from your Microsoft 365 and line-of-business datasets. Work IQ also includes Copilot Memory to better understand your style, habits, and preferences. The result is a rich experience that is implicitly grounded in the details of your work patterns, relationships, and workflows. It enables Copilot to provide new insights and anticipate future needs and proactively take action to deliver the best results.

What are agents and what can they do?
Agents are AI systems that help individuals and organizations manage tasks and make informed decisions. Using agents within Copilot, you and your business can drive efficiency and get more work done faster. Agents can perform various tasks to assist people and organizations:
Retrieve information: Query systems like HR, CRM, or financial databases.
Help with basic tasks: Submit expense reports, update CRM systems, create IT help desk tickets.
Perform specialized roles: Facilitate meetings, compile research reports, analyze data sets.
Automate processes: Handle financial planning, sales qualification, and supplier communications.

What’s the difference between Copilot Studio in Microsoft Copilot and Copilot Studio licensed separately?
Copilot Studio that’s included with a Microsoft 365 Copilot license lets the licensed users build and use internal agents within Microsoft 365 using the Copilot Chat and Standard harness at no additional cost.
The standalone Copilot Studio license offers additional flexibility—you can create agents using the GitHub Copilot harness and publish to external channels (like websites, apps, and social platforms), non-licensed users can use the agent, and you can choose from different pricing options.
